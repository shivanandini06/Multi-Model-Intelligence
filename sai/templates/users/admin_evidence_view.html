Why digests matter

A SHA-256 digest is a fixed-length fingerprint of a file's exact bytes. It cannot be reverse-engineered, and changing a single pixel of a QR image produces a completely different digest. Storing it alongside the artefact lets anyone later prove that the file they are looking at is the exact file that was assessed — which is what turns a screenshot into admissible evidence.
